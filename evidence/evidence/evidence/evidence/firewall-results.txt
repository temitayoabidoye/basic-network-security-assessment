Security Assessment:

This indicates that restrictive inbound rules were not observed in the inspected chain. It does not prove that no other firewall or network-level security controls exist.

Recommendation:
Review the complete firewall configuration and implement a restrictive inbound policy where appropriate. Explicitly allow only required services and restrict administrative access.
